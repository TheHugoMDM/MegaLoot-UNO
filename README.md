Changelog.
Version 0.3
-Completed Samurai and knight armors (Models and textures).
-Added new ore (common shard) can be found under Y14.
-Added new structure (can be found in the highest hills).
-Added a use for the Rift stone (can re-roll the infused emeralds).
	-Changed, recipe for the Rift Stone.
-Added a new feature for upgrade charms (now you can remove effects with it, just rename it to the effect's number in your tool/armor/weapon/ring).
-Added hopper compatibility to the Evolution chamber and the Disenchanter.
	-Added the option automatic mode to the Disenchanter (auto disenchant).
-Added a switch to the jetpack effect (can be turned on/off by pressing the activate key [check keybinds]).
	-Changed, some improvements to the jetpack movement.
	-Added particles to the Jetpack flying.
-Added, Now you can change the type of loot you get from the weaponcases, (Crouch + Right Click) > ONLY CREATIVE <
-Changed, disenchanter and evolution chamber are faster. 
-Changed, loot rebalance to the stats (they have a "common" stats range, each extra point above the "common max" has a 200% harder to be applied
										                   that means you can get a 50 damage sword but its very extremely rare to get, and a 51 damage is even more)
-Changed, now when you upgrade something that already has all the effects but still has upgrades, you add a extra point to the main stat (damage, mining speed, armor or power)
-Changed, increased multishot arrow's spread. 
-Fixed, Swords dealing wrong display damage.
-Fixed, Swords dealing a lot more damage when armor is equipped.
